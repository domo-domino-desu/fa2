Roadwork Ahead: Downtime, Minor Updates, New Banner (G)

2 days ago

Hey there, Fuzzies!


Phew, the past couple of weeks have been bumpy. Our Tech team has been working overtime to continue site maintenance. Thank you everyone for your patience. 🫶


What we have done since the last journal:

⚙️ Bug fixes in relation to duped favorites and watches.
⚙️ Addition of Retro Light theme based on user feedback.
⚙️ Users now have visible UUIDs for permanent linkage.
⚙️ Various optimizations to make the site more snappy.



Retro Light Theme



Retro Light theme has been added to the list of site themes in your settings. This theme closely resembles Classic Light Mode. Please give it a look, and leave us feedback in a ticket.

As we move along with the Dev Friendly Initiative, we are getting closer to the time where Classic will be retired. We promised we would have a Retro Light theme available before that date comes.



UUIDs and you!



With the API coming soon, we’ve exposed the new Universally Unique User Identifiers, or “UUIDs”, that will be utilized to provide a more permanent identifier for each user beyond their username. And before you ask: yes, this is a step towards name changes. The UUIDs will be unchanging, so feel free to use them for things like QR codes and whatnot!

What even is a UUID and why are we using them? It is an ID assigned to your account that is guaranteed to be unique. If you generated trillions of UUIDs every second, it would still take multiple years to generate a duplicate.

You are unable to tag another user with their UUID for now, so please continue using usernames for icon embedding and other codes found in our Tags and Codes guide.



Server errors, Slowdowns, What’s it all for?



We know the past two weeks have been frustrating. In previous journals we mentioned that with our tech team focusing on the Dev Friendly Initiative, it would look like a whole lot of nothing was happening. Unfortunately, some of our background maintenance became foreground maintenance.

We have been doing work on the database to help improve site functionality and to continue forward with modernizing the site’s codebase and infrastructure. This work needs to be done before we can finish implementing heavily requested features such as API, username changes, better blocking, 2-factor authentication, etc.


We really can’t thank you enough for your patience while we undergo these growing pains.



Upcoming Maintenance



Between November 21, 2026 12:00:00 AM to 05:00:00 AM, we will be undergoing site maintenance. The website will be offline and inaccessible during this time.

The times above are automatically converted to your local time.



Banner Change



Rednef’s currently out picking up her quad shot, pumpkin spice frappe with extra whip and cinnamon, so I’ll be unveiling our latest addition to the Community Banner gallery!

This new adorable banner has been made by  Rhari! You can favorite the original banner here.⭐COMMUNITY BANNER ENTRY INFO⭐


Banner entries are open to the entire community! Please add FABanner2026 to the keywords of your submission so we can easily locate entries!


What are community banners?

Our Community Banners are where artists submit banner ideas throughout the year and, if chosen, they are featured as the site's default banner for a minimum of one week—sometimes longer if it fits a season or holiday. There is no set amount of banners that we choose. We may pick 3, or 12, or even 42! Remember to add FABanner2026 to your keywords for us to easily locate submissions. We ask that at least one of the mascots is featured, but preferably both of us. Focus must be primarily on the mascots. All banners must meet General Rating guidelines to be considered. Banner sizes are fixed at 1850x300 pixels.


Thank you so much again,  Rhari! 🫶We hope everyone had a fun Halloween. Please keep the comments on topic, and stay excellent to each other.
