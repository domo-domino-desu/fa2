Roadwork Ahead: Downtime, Minor Updates, New Banner
News & Updates | General | Posted 2 days ago
Hey there, Fuzzies!


Phew, the past couple of weeks have been bumpy. Our Tech team has been working overtime to continue site maintenance. Thank you everyone for your patience. 🫶


What we have done since the last journal:

⚙️ Bug fixes in relation to duped favorites and watches.
⚙️ Addition of Retro Light theme based on user feedback.
⚙️ Users now have visible UUIDs for permanent linkage.
⚙️ Various optimizations to make the site more snappy.



Retro Light Theme



Retro Light theme has been added to the list of site themes in your settings. This theme closely resembles Classic Light Mode. Please give it a look, and leave us feedback in a ticket.

As we move along with the Dev Friendly Initiative, we are getting closer to the time where Classic will be retired. We promised we would have a Retro Light theme available before that date comes.



UUIDs and you!



With the API coming soon, we’ve exposed the new Universally Unique User Identifiers, or “UUIDs”, that will be utilized to provide a more permanent identifier for each user beyond their username. And before you ask: yes, this is a step towards name changes. The UUIDs will be unchanging, so feel free to use them for things like QR codes and whatnot!

What even is a UUID and why are we using them? It is an ID assigned to your account that is guaranteed to be unique. If you generated trillions of UUIDs every second, it would still take multiple years to generate a duplicate.

You are unable to tag another user with their UUID for now, so please continue using usernames for icon embedding and other codes found in our Tags and Codes guide.



Server errors, Slowdowns, What’s it all for?



We know the past two weeks have been frustrating. In previous journals we mentioned that with our tech team focusing on the Dev Friendly Initiative, it would look like a whole lot of nothing was happening. Unfortunately, some of our background maintenance became foreground maintenance.

We have been doing work on the database to help improve site functionality and to continue forward with modernizing the site’s codebase and infrastructure. This work needs to be done before we can finish implementing heavily requested features such as API, username changes, better blocking, 2-factor authentication, etc.


We really can’t thank you enough for your patience while we undergo these growing pains.



Upcoming Maintenance



Between November 21, 2026 12:00:00 AM to 05:00:00 AM, we will be undergoing site maintenance. The website will be offline and inaccessible during this time.

The times above are automatically converted to your local time.

Oct 28th - Post Downtime and Session Expirations
News & Updates | General | Posted 3 weeks ago
Hey there, Fuzzies!

Wow, our Tech team has been busy this past week! (Send them coffee ☕ and a nap.) Here’s a quick recap of what we’ve done since the last journal.

Post-Downtime Updates


We survived another downtime and finalized all changes needed to prevent exploits related to CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. The undesirable side effect of this was some site instability due to upgrading from PHP 7.4 to 8.5.12, and the Tech team has been tirelessly working on fixes and mitigating poor user experience. One known issue is the missing IPs on the activity logs, which are cosmetic for the time being and should be restored soon.

Summary of changes since Wednesday 21st, 2026:
⚙️ - Upgraded from PHP 7.4 to 8.5.12.
⚙️ - Known issue of IPs missing from the Activity Log that will be restored soon.
⚙️ - Taco recipe relocated in relation to CVE-2026-67420.


Session Expirations



As our community grows, so does the need to approach more modern security methods. One of these will be the expirations of session cookies after six (6) months, which is down from our previous expiration: the heat death of our universe.


The intent is twofold: security and scalability. Though the chance of exploiting our sessions system via cookies to allow unauthorized access to an account is nigh impossible, it's not zero, and that keeps the security side of our tech team up at night. This, coupled with the sheer number of sessions we have to track forever, makes for a headache of a combo.

Over the next few days, login sessions that are older than six (6) months will be cleared, leading to users being logged out. This is normal, and your account is not compromised. If you are concerned that you were compromised, please review the Active Session page and contact fixture@example.invalid.

Continuing Slowdowns



Expect the occasional slow-down as we continue to work behind the scenes. This should resolve within the week. We’re sorry for any inconvenience and thank you for your patience

Phew, maintenance is over! The past few days have been a whirlwind, and we can’t thank the community enough. 😭This journal is going to be a little long, so please buckle up!


Post-Update Recap



We go more in-depth into everything here further along in the journal.
⚙️- Database has been migrated from MySQL 5.7 to 8.4
⚙️- User Flairs have been added. (Modern only)
⚙️- Avatar Decorations have been added for beta testers. (Modern only)
⚙️- Advertiser portal for FA Community Ads is live. If you are a previous Advertiser, check your e-mail for a message from our Ad Team!

Want beta access so you can have Avatar Decorations before they go live for everyone? Sign up for FA+ and you can try them out today! Note: Avatar Decorations are in early testing and there will very likely be some eventual changes to sizing before they go live for everyone.

This update marks a very important milestone for our continued work on our Dev Friendly Initiative and modernizing our codebase. We have only video embedding and the API left to implement, and then we will be locking down and focusing our Tech team to mostly back end work.

GoFundMe Livestream Recap



https://www.twitch.tv/videos/2893126845


Highlights from the Livestream:

Funding

Thanks to all of you, we have been funded for at least the next eight (8) months. 💙 This means that third-party advertising is now deactivated site-wide! We may still participate in affiliate programs if they meet our standards. These will be run on our in-house infrastructure.


The Hosting Bill

Our Tech lead,  kaisa, explained what went into our hosting bill in the livestream. The explanation starts at the 52 minute mark. The cost breaks down as follows:
◦ - The colocation is 1 full rack, 45 U including the networking and datacenter infrastructure
◦ - 6 Bare-metal servers + networking and utilities at ~40 U
◦ - Blended IP service with layer 2 DDOS protection
◦ - Service is a managed hosting solution and the datacenter staff is contracted to help us with general maintenance on the system such as system upgrades and drive expansions. They also assist us with goal planning and infrastructure upgrades when required.


The team at the datacenter are fantastic partners that are extremely excited about what we are offering and we are very fortunate to have a good relationship with them.


Email Services

At the 56 minute mark we bring up email costs.
◦ - Our email cost varies by usage.
◦ - We use a hosted service and need dedicated IPs to control our email reputation.
◦ - Our email used to cost us over $200 a month, but we were able to make adjustments to bring the pricing down to under $125 a month.
We’ve also have been approached with other options to consider since this recording to bring costs down even more. We will be fully exploring these to see which might best fit our needs!


Plans for Future Funding

This starts at the 59 minute mark.
◦ - Make sure users are aware of FA+ even existing.
◦ - Look at higher FA+ tiers and/or PWYW tiers.
◦ - Support in-house so that the process of taking out an ad is easier.
◦ - Make folks aware of the shop.
◦ - Adding a profile decoration section to the shop to bring in steady funding.


FA+ Numbers

We know you are curious, so here come the numbers from the livestream. Before our funding drive announcement, we had 2,880 active FA+ subscribers. As of the livestream, we have 7,085 active subscribers. Thank you!
We loved chatting with everyone!

In-House Advertising Changes


Like we mentioned in the livestream, there will be some changes to in-house advertising in order to bring focus back to all of you. You can find that information below!


Lil Banner Changes

Originally, we had changed Lil banners from 300 x 90 to 320 x 50 at the request of third-party advertisers. The change was abruptly put upon us, and as a result, we had to put it on our in-house just as suddenly. In-House hated it, and the main feedback was that you felt it was too difficult to showcase your art at this size.
We agree and will be moving in-house to 320 x 100. It is still an IAB standard size, but it is not as common as the 320 x 50 mobile banner.
There is no deadline to send us new banners if you have an active campaign. The site will be able to display both old and new sizes at the same time until the old are phased out.
To change your banners, please do so on the Ad Portal. You will need to import your campaign.


Ad Portal

Yes, you read right! Thanks to our wonderful Tech Team, we now have an Ad Portal!
You will be able to do the following on the portal:
◦ - Import an existing campaign.
◦ - Start a new campaign.
◦ - Renew a campaign.
◦ - Pay for your campaign or receive an invoice link.
◦ - Change out your banners.
We know that the old system of going by the same day number of the month would mean getting ads on shorter months like February meant fewer days overall. The portal now counts ad time based on 30-day increments.
If you need technical assistance with the Ad Portal, please contact our Advertising Admin at fixture@example.invalid.
If you have paid for a campaign, but are waiting for it to go live: Please wait until you have received your campaign confirmation email from Advertising before importing your campaign into the portal.

We will continue to add more profile decorations as we go along. If you have any suggestions for either a border or a flair, feel free to let us know! Previously our staff members were marked with a shield icon to the left of their username. We’ve moved away from that icon and instead we now have our rainbow splat logo! All staff members will have this on the left side of their username. No splat? Not a staff account.

Support Fur Affinity’s Community Funding
News & Updates | General | Posted a month ago

Edit: We have moved downtime!


Downtime has been moved to October 9, 2026 01:00:00 AM - 11:00:00 AM.

⭐Gofund Me ⭐
Moved to the top at user request.


Hey there Fuzzies!

Recently, there has been a lot of conversation about censorship of the internet and third parties forcing sites to make changes to their content allowances or face financial penalties. We are sad to say that Fur Affinity has not been immune to this pressure. While we used to be able to use third-party advertisers to help fund the site, they are now bringing in less than $150 USD a month. For reference, the cost to keep Fur Affinity running is over $25,000 USD per month (there’s a breakdown further into the post, for those curious)!

Over the past couple of years we have been working with our third-party advertisers to meet their demands on content moderation. This has not resulted in any changes to our policies, but we have had to shift more staff focus to rating change tickets (which unfortunately slows down our response times in other categories). Some users may have noticed they received multiple ratings change notifications in a short span of time. This is a result of more submissions in violation of General rating guidelines being reported to us combined with more staff focusing on that specific area. And just to be clear: Content that does not break our rules has no action taken against it, even if it is reported.

This effort has cost us in resources and time in the hopes of having this source of funding restored completely. Despite jumping through every hoop presented to us, we have had our income severely restricted with one demand to remove the restriction: remove all of what they consider to be “adult content”, which we refuse to do.

To reiterate: We will not be removing adult content from Fur Affinity.

What the third-party advertisers consider adult isn’t even always actual adult content. For further context here’s a snippet from our Fur Affinity Advertising Manager,  Eden Vale

Howdy!

What makes this situation frustrating is that the bar for what advertisers consider “adult content” is very very low. For example they consider plus size characters, two characters hugging, and “barbie doll nudity” to be adult content, despite these being normal everyday things that are not adult at all. This is why you may see other sites with restrictions on how characters can’t be posed or dressed “too provocatively.”

We receive a daily list of items that they have flagged as violations and have to sort through them. If they’re legitimate violations, we re-rate them. If they aren’t, we don’t touch them. Submissions that are not violations of our policies are still held against us.

We have had many email conversations, Zoom calls, and even an in-person meeting with representatives to help resolve this to no avail.

Given this uphill (and frankly annoying) struggle, if properly funded we would like to aim towards removing third-party advertising entirely, and instead focus on our in-house community advertisers. They are the heart and soul of the fandom, and they deserve to have the eyes of our active users.

And here with a follow up is Fur Affinity’s Site Owner  Momma Sciggles

With more and more sites restricting the content they allow, we believe it’s important to maintain our current site standings to protect the artistic freedom of our talented community. However, this comes with a hefty burden of now needing other ways to fund the site. Third-party advertisers aren’t entirely off the table at the moment, but as the funding from them has been significantly lowered, they cannot be relied upon as heavily as they were before. They want us to play by their rules; we have high traffic that they would love to get ads in front of. But we know that censoring Fur Affinity to meet their standards for unrestricted funding isn’t healthy for the site or the community.

For the sake of transparency, these are the monthly costs (in USD) for the site (note—the funds are specific but we will not be saying who we contract and host with for their privacy and protection, they are good beans):

Contracted staff/coders: *$15k

Extra: ***??? Varies

This number is split in different ways between 7 individuals that dedicate their days to the harder parts of managing Fur Affinity, such as: managing code, handling advertising, urgent emails and meetings, legal obligations and reports, and so many more high-level management tasks. More than Trouble Tickets and not being paid nearly their worth for the work they do and still very much volunteer for the rest of their time. These roles are vital to keeping FA online and are more than what one person would be able to handle on their own.

**This is based off of a percentage of sales on Paypal. We were forced to take out a loan when a third-party initially pulled out in June 2025 and hoped we would be able to resolve and bounce back from the issue to not need to ask for community help at the time. Trust me that I wish we had not gone that route...

***This does not include any emergency funds needed or refilling retainers with our lawyers. Those are difficult to calculate in a monthly breakdown as they are more of an “as-needed” expense. It is usually $5k at a time when we are due to refill the retainer. Unfortunately we’ve needed to refill it often as we’ve needed help bringing FA into compliance with the ever-changing modern day regulations and legal standards. As for “emergency” funds, we would ideally like $20-30k set aside. “Emergency” in this case would basically be anything related to physical hardware issues.

We are 3 months behind on some of these items and are grateful to our hosts for being understanding and willing to give us such grace. We had hoped to resolve these issues with third-party advertising to catch up but we now know that isn’t going to be a proper resolution for this. We must instead reach out to the community for assistance if we are to keep things going. This has been difficult to come to terms with and try to approach. Internet Safety and Scamming



We want to address some concerns with internet safety and scamming, especially with the upcoming holidays which typically have an increase in suspicious and fraudulent activities.

Whether you use Fur Affinity, Discord, Bluesky, Reddit, Telegram or any other form of social media, furries by and large have been seeing a rise in attempts for commission and art scams. While our moderation team works behind the scenes to stop them, the only way to truly stop scammers is with the knowledge of their existence and tactics. We have seen a very small, but persistent group continue to try to cause issues, and we want to remind our users of some safety best practices.

Common Examples of Scams



– Staff Impersonation Scams: We have recently seen an increase in scammers pretending to be site staff. Fur Affinity staff will never ask you to change your email to something that is not yours, nor will we ask you to give us your password. In addition, our staff will not contact you about matters related to the website or your account in the Fur Affinity Discord or as a private message on Discord. Legitimate notifications from Fur Affinity Staff will always appear in one of two forms:
– Notes on site from the official official "Fur Affinity Staff" account (look for the star shield icon before the display name, like the one next to my name, and double-check the account name).

– An email from an [at]furaffinity.net address.

Fur Affinity staff will never...
– Contact you and ask you to change your password.
– Contact you and ask you to change your email to a different email which you do not own.
– Discuss an action or possible action for a user through a third party.
– Contact you through another platform [Discord, Twitter, Bluesky] about an issue occurring on our site.
– Contact you as an individual staff member in a direct message (DM) to you on Discord, another platform, or on our site.

Users with questions, comments, or concerns about moderation actions should open a Trouble Ticket or send an email via appeals.

– YCH Scams:
One of the more common scams is the claim that an older YCH (“your character here”) commission has suddenly become available again after the original buyer backed out. They target previous highest bidders on YCHs in an attempt to dupe them into paying. Most of the time, they use new accounts with a name that is very close to the original artist’s username, but the spelling/formatting is just slightly off.

– Commission Scammers:
If someone messages you first with an unsolicited commission offer, that is a red flag. Scammers love to impersonate existing artists via stolen or AI-generated content to lure people in with the promise of affordable, high-quality commissions with fast turnaround times. They then send out mass notices in an attempt to catch as many people as they can. This type of scammer will often request for further communication to continue on another platform, such as Discord or Telegram. If somebody messages first, always be wary and do your research before committing to a commission.

– "Accidentally Reported You" Scams:
Another common tactic is the scammer pretending they've reported your account, then telling you what to do to make sure action isn't taken against you. The directions and links they give are malicious and should not be followed. If you're ever unsure about the status of your account please open a Trouble Ticket and staff will assist you.

Protecting Yourself



– Invoice When Paying:
Always ask for an invoice, and NEVER pay with “friends and family” options unless you know and trust the person. Doing so waives your rights to a refund and any money sent is considered a non-refundable gift. Again, always request an invoice; they come with buyer protections built in.

– Double-Check Their Profile:
Take a moment to double-check that the person you are messaging is who they say they are. New accounts will have a green leaf icon in place after their display name.



Ensure they are not using a new account, and their profile takes you to a user page that has been registered for a while and has an established gallery. Galleries without a visible history of completed works can be a red flag. Be cautious if you receive a message from an account you do not know or appears new, especially if you get an unfamiliar link encouraging you to click it.

– Double-Check Their Username:
While display names can have duplicates, usernames cannot. Make sure to check the username, not just the display name! What are community banners?

Artists submit banner ideas throughout the year and, if chosen, they will be featured as the site's default banner for a minimum of one week—sometimes longer if it fits a season or holiday. There is no set amount of banners that we choose. We may pick 3, or 12, or even 42!

Remember to add FABanner2025 to your tags for us to easily locate submissions. We ask that at least one of the mascots is featured, but preferably both of us. Focus must be primarily on the mascots. All banners must meet General Rating guidelines to be considered. Banner sizes are fixed at 1850x300 pixels.
